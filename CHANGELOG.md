# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the middle number
moves for new behaviour, the last for fixes alone. The newest version goes at
the top; a release is refused if its version has no section here.

## 0.1.1

- **Bug fixes:** We fixed a few bugs to keep things running smooth.

## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Upgrades in your bags:** a green arrow on any item that's better than what you wear. The tooltip says how much better. A Put It On button shows when a new upgrade lands in your bags.
- **Quest rewards:** a Take button picks the biggest upgrade for you.
- **Where to get upgrades:** every upgrade for each slot from vendors, quests, dungeons, raids and crafting.
- **Dungeons:** Dungeon Loot shows what each boss drops with your upgrades marked, and Dungeon Quests lists every quest done inside a dungeon.
- **Upgrade Quest:** pick your next upgrade and follow a step by step guide, with an arrow.
- **Your way:** change the stat weights for your class and spec, and see which class spells you can train.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
